# Magnet OS — Employee User Manual

Welcome to **Magnet OS**, our agency operating system. This guide covers everything you need to work in the system day to day.

**System link:** https://magnet-op.vercel.app

---

## 1. Getting Started

### Logging in
1. Open the system link.
2. Enter your **username (or email)** and **password** (your admin gives you these).
3. First time? Go to **Profile → Change Password** and set your own password.

### What happens automatically when you log in
- ✅ You're marked **active** in the **Activity Log**.
- ✅ Your **attendance is recorded** for the day (check-in time) — no manual punch needed.
- When you **log out**, your **check-out time** is saved automatically.

### Background music 🎵
- Top bar has a **♫ button** — toggle the system background music on/off anytime. Your choice is remembered.

---

## 2. Finding Your Way Around

- **Left sidebar** (desktop): your modules, grouped (Main, Sales, Finance, Team/HR, etc.).
- **Bottom bar + "More"** (mobile): tap **More** to reach every module.
- **Top bar**: music toggle, Cloud/Local status, 🔔 notifications, your profile.
- You only see the modules your **role** allows. If something's missing, that's by design (ask your manager).

---

## 3. Your Daily Work

### Dashboard
Your home screen — a summary of your tasks, deadlines, and what needs attention.

### Tasks ✅
- **Board view** (like Trello): drag cards between **To Do → In Progress → Done**.
- **Click a card** to open it: change status, add a **checklist**, see details.
- **Add a task:** **+ New task**. **Delete:** open the task → **Delete** (or **Del** in List view).
- Finishing tasks earns you **points** 🏆 (see Gamification).
- 🔒 If you don't have edit permission, the board is **read-only** (you can view but not move cards).

### Deliverables 🎬
- The production board for client work: **Internal Review → Client Review → Approved**.
- Submit your work, add versions, and request/approve revisions where allowed.

### Boards in general (Trello-style)
- **Labels** (colored), **due dates**, **checklists**, and a **detail drawer** when you click a card.
- On mobile the drawer opens as a **bottom sheet**; boards **swipe** between columns.

### Content Calendar 🗓️
Scheduled content per client, with status and approvals.

---

## 4. Requests & Approvals

- **Requests:** submit a request (leave, expense, tool access, approval, etc.).
- **Leaves:** request time off; your manager approves/declines.
- **Approval Center** (managers): review and approve pending items.
- You'll get a **notification** when your request is actioned.

---

## 5. Communication

- **🔔 Notifications:** top-bar bell. Filter **All / Unread / Mine**; updates live. Click to jump to the item.
- **Chat:** team channels for quick coordination.
- **Activity Log:** a record of who did what and when.

---

## 6. Clients, Sales & Briefs (if your role has access)

- **Leads / CRM:** the sales pipeline — drag leads across stages.
- **Campaigns:** marketing forms that capture leads straight into the CRM.
- **Client Briefs:** generate a brief link, send it to a client (Copy / WhatsApp); their answers come back into the system as **Submitted**, fully viewable.
- **Clients / Projects:** client profiles, projects, and their tasks/deliverables.

---

## 7. Gamification 🏆

- Earn **points** for real work: finishing tasks, sending work to clients, approvals, delivery.
- Collect **badges** ⚡🔥⭐ and keep a **daily streak**.
- **Leaderboard** (under *Fun*): see the team ranking + **Magnet Run** and **Arcade** game results.
- **Arcade / Magnet Run:** quick break games — your scores show on the leaderboard.

---

## 8. Your Profile & Account

- **Profile:** update your details, change your password.
- **Email verification:** if you get a verification link, click it to activate your account.
- Keep your password private. Never share your login.

---

## 9. Mobile Tips 📱

- Works like an app — add it to your home screen (**Add to Home Screen**) for one-tap access.
- Use the **bottom bar** + **More** sheet to navigate.
- Tables turn into easy-to-read cards; buttons are touch-sized.
- If you don't see a recent change, do a **hard refresh** (close the tab and reopen).

---

## 10. Quick FAQ

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Forgot my password? | Use **Forgot password** on the login screen, or ask your admin to reset it. |
| A module is missing? | Your role may not include it — ask your manager. |
| My change isn't showing for a teammate? | Make sure you're both online; data syncs live within seconds. |
| Can't move a card on a board? | You likely have read-only access to that board. |
| Do I need to clock in/out? | No — login records attendance, logout records check-out automatically. |

---

## 11. Golden Rules

1. **Change your default password** on day one.
2. **Keep tasks updated** — move them as you progress; it keeps everyone aligned.
3. **Check notifications** daily.
4. **Submit requests** through the system (not chat) so they're tracked and approved properly.
5. Log out at the end of your day so your **attendance closes** correctly.
