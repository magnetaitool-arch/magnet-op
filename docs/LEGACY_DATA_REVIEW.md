# Legacy data review

Source: reconciled production release snapshot, 2026-09-26. No records changed. IDs are lookup references; contact values, credentials and private financial details are intentionally excluded. These are business decisions, not a migration retry list.

## Decision rules

- CONTACT: a shared email may be a shared inbox, a repeated inquiry, or a duplicate identity. Preserve separate records unless the owner confirms one identity; link rather than delete. Cases in the same group need one coordinated decision.
- ASSET: an active child references an archived parent. The child may need archiving, the parent may have been archived accidentally, or the relationship may be wrong. Confirm intent before restoring/relinking/archiving.
- IDENTITY: no confirmed canonical mapping. Confirm the person and their existing Auth identity; do not infer from a similar name or email.
- FINANCE: a legacy payment cannot resolve a valid invoice. Confirm the original invoice or classify the payment explicitly; never manufacture an invoice or alter money values.
- STORAGE: an object outside the document catalog may belong to another legitimate tool. Confirm owner/purpose before cataloging; leave bytes in place.
- TASK: unresolved project relationship. Identify the intended project and assignee, or explicitly retain as archived history. Never choose the first matching project. Historical issue flags for relationships already repaired are excluded.
