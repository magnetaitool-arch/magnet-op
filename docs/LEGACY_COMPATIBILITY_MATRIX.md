## Deprecation decision rule

A legacy source can be deprecated only after explicit old→new identity mappings,
complete representative staging rehearsal, per-field/count/link reconciliation,
least-privilege workflow tests, production post-migration verification and a tested
rollback window. Zero rows or successful DDL alone never satisfy the rule.
Discrepancies receive immutable review records; preserve original values and reviewer
provenance. Do not infer employee/user, client/contact, tenant or financial identity
from matching names/email/phone. Continue unrelated safe repairs while reviews remain open.

## Immediate dispositions

- Removed the automatic browser retention caller; this preserves data rather than
  changing its meaning. The server pruning RPC is not yet removed and needs an
  independently reviewed retention/privilege migration.
- Lead conversion now accepts explicit relationship evidence only. Contact matches,
  conflicting links and deleted/unavailable linked clients require review, with no
  mutation. Atomic server-side conversion remains required; this guard is not RLS.
- Signup and upload authorization repair migrations remain local/staging candidates.
- The initial 19 structural/mapping findings plus 32 shared-contact candidates are a review queue, not 19 proven corrupt records. A
  historical record pointing to a deleted parent may be valid and must not be relinked
  or deleted merely to make a report green.
